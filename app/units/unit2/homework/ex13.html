Challenge. Read the number of guests and how many slices each guest eats. A pizza has 8 slices and costs 45 shekels.

Print how many pizzas to order (rounded up), the total cost, and how many slices are left over:

Guests: 5
Slices per guest: 3
Pizzas: 2
Cost: 90
Leftover slices: 1

Rules:

  • Write at least two helper methods below main (for example: pizzasNeeded, leftover).
  • No if statements: round up using integer division only. Hint: what does (slices + 7) / 8 give for 15, 16 and 17 slices?
