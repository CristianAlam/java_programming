2. Algorithms

An algorithm is a finite, precise, ordered list of steps that solves a task. A recipe is a good everyday example.

Precise
Every step is clear, with no guessing
Ordered
The order of the steps matters
Finite
It ends eventually

Example: making tea

  1. Fill the kettle with water.
  2. Turn on the kettle and wait until it boils.
  3. Put a tea bag in a cup.
  4. Pour the boiling water into the cup.
  5. Wait 3 minutes.
  6. Remove the tea bag.
Think about it
What happens if you swap steps 3 and 6? What if step 2 just said "heat the water"? Computers are very literal: they do exactly what you say, not what you meant.

How to approach a task

Analyze (what is given? what is required?) → Algorithm (plain words) → Implement (write code) → Run & test → Fix (and repeat)
