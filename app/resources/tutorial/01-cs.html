1. What is Computer Science?

Computer science is about solving problems with computation. It isn't really "about computers", any more than astronomy is about telescopes.

Navigation
Find the shortest route between two cities
Medicine
Spot tumors in thousands of scan images
Biology
Compare DNA sequences billions of letters long
Finance
Detect credit-card fraud in real time
Games
Build a computer opponent that plays chess well

All of these need two things: a clear method for solving the problem (an algorithm) and a way to tell a computer to carry it out (a program).
